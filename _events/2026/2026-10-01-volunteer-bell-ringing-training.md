## Get Ready to Ring: Volunteer Bell Ringer Training Coming to Lake Isabella

As the holiday season approaches, local community members have an opportunity to make a direct impact in the Kern River Valley. The Salvation Army is hosting a **Volunteer Bell Ringer Training** session to prepare volunteers for the annual Red Kettle campaign.

Whether you are a returning volunteer or looking to ring the bell for the first time, this training session will cover everything needed for the upcoming season, including shift scheduling, kettle setup, site guidelines, and best practices for engaging with community donors.

### Why Volunteer?

Bell ringers are the backbone of the Red Kettle initiative. Funds raised during the holiday season directly support local community assistance programs, helping provide food, emergency aid, and holiday support to families in need throughout the area. 

### Who Should Attend?

This training is open to individuals, families, civic groups, and local business teams interested in taking shifts during the holiday campaign.
